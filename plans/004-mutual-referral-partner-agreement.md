# 004 — Mutual Referral Partner Agreement Template

**Status:** ready to build, pending 2 decisions (see Open Decisions)
**Deliverable:** native Google Doc, `Eagle Ridge - Mutual Referral Partner Agreement Template`

## Summary

A mutual, non-exclusive, **referral-only** agreement template. Not a reseller or
channel agreement. It defines what counts as a valid referral, an accepted
referral, eligible revenue, and a payable fee — with explicit deliverables and
acceptance criteria on both sides.

Header note on page 1: *template for counsel review, not legal advice.*

## Resolved Decisions

1. **Fee direction:** two-way. Schedule A has Part 1 (ER pays Partner) and Part 2 (Partner pays ER).
2. **Entity:** Humaine Studio LLC, a Delaware limited liability company doing business as Eagle Ridge Advisory. Governing law: Commonwealth of Virginia.

## Agreement Body

### 1. Nature of Relationship
- Mutual, non-exclusive referral rights. Neither party owes the other any minimum
  volume, and neither is obligated to accept any referral.
- Independent contractors. No partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment.
- **Referral-only.** Neither party may act as a reseller, distributor, or
  sales agent unless separately authorized in a signed writing.

### 2. Partner Restrictions
Partner may not: bind Eagle Ridge; quote prices, discounts, or terms; promise
outcomes; guarantee certification or assessment results; use Eagle Ridge
branding without prior written approval; or represent itself as a reseller.

### 3. Partner Referral Deliverables
For each referral the Partner provides:
qualified introduction · prospect contact details · source of relationship ·
stated business need · timing · **consent confirmation** · conflict and
prohibited-party disclosure.

### 4. Eagle Ridge Deliverable
Written acceptance or rejection within `[Response Window: default 5 business days]`.
A rejection notice must state the reason. For a "already in pipeline" rejection,
Eagle Ridge represents it holds dated records predating the submission.

### 5. Acceptance Criteria
A referral is **accepted** only if all of the following hold:
- Eagle Ridge confirms in writing and assigns a Referral ID.
- The submission identifies the prospect well enough to check against pipeline.
- The prospect consented to the introduction.
- The Partner is legally permitted to receive referral compensation.
- No prohibited payee is in the payment chain (see §8).

### 6. Customer Deliverables — Out of Scope
Eagle Ridge customer deliverables, milestones, and acceptance terms live **only**
in the customer SOW or engagement letter. The Partner has no right to approve,
reject, direct, or dispute Eagle Ridge customer work, and no fee is contingent on
customer acceptance of that work.

### 7. Non-Qualifying Referrals
No fee is owed for: rejected referrals · accounts already in pipeline ·
duplicate submissions · prior contacts · public RFP responses · existing
customers · referrals made without prospect consent.

### 8. Prohibited Payees
No fee may flow to: a government official or employee · a procurement
decision-maker for the referred customer · an employee or agent of the referred
customer · a regulated professional barred from referral compensation by their
licensing body · any party on a US restricted-party list.

### 9. Compliance
- **Disclosure.** Partner discloses the paid referral relationship to the
  prospect before or at the time of introduction.
- **Anti-kickback.** Partner represents the fee is not tied to any federal
  contract or subcontract in violation of the Anti-Kickback Act
  (41 U.S.C. §8701 et seq.), and that no portion flows to a government official.
- **CMMC conflict of interest.** If the Partner is (or becomes) a C3PAO, RPO, CCP,
  or CCA engaged to assess a referred customer, it must notify Eagle Ridge
  promptly. Fees for that customer are void from the date the assessment
  engagement begins. This is a **continuing** obligation, not a one-time rep.
- **Regulated professionals.** Partner represents its profession's rules permit
  receipt of referral compensation, and it has made any required client disclosure.

### 10. Term, Termination, Survival
- Initial term `[Term: default 1 year]`, auto-renewing for successive 1-year terms.
- Either party may terminate for convenience on `[Notice: default 30 days]` written notice.
- **Survival: fees remain payable on referrals accepted before the termination
  date, for the full fee tail.** Termination does not cut off the tail.
- Sections 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12 and Schedule A payment obligations survive.

### 11. Confidentiality & Non-Solicit
Mutual confidentiality for prospect data and commercial terms. Neither party
solicits the other's employees for `[Non-Solicit: default 12 months]`; general
job postings are not solicitation.

### 12. General
Limitation of liability (each party's aggregate liability capped at fees paid or
payable in the prior 12 months; no consequential damages) · governing law and
venue `[State]` · no assignment without consent, except to a successor in
interest · notices to `[Notice Email — Eagle Ridge]` and `[Notice Email — Partner]` ·
entire agreement · amendment only in signed writing.

### 13. Signature Blocks
Both parties: entity name, signatory name, title, date.

## Schedule A — Commercial Terms

**Part 1 — Eagle Ridge pays Partner**

- **Fee:** `[Referral Fee %: default 10%]` of Eligible Revenue.
- **Eligible Revenue:** amounts **actually collected** by Eagle Ridge from the
  accepted referred customer, **excluding** taxes, refunds, chargebacks, credits,
  reimbursed expenses, and third-party pass-through costs.
- **Fee tail:** revenue collected during `[Fee Tail: default 12 months]` from the
  date of the **first paid customer agreement** with that customer.
- **Scope within the tail:** applies to any SOW or engagement with that customer
  signed during the tail, including expansions. **No fee on renewals or new work
  beginning after the tail expires.**
- **Relationship to registration expiry:** registration expiry (Schedule B)
  governs *whether a lead converts to an accepted referral*. Once accepted and a
  first paid agreement is signed, the fee tail runs independently and is not
  shortened by expiry.
- **Payout cadence:** quarterly, within `[Payment Window: default 30 days after
  quarter-end]`, after both cash collection and receipt of a valid Partner invoice.
- **Refunds/clawback:** a refund or chargeback after payout creates a credit
  against future referral payments, or a repayment obligation if no future
  payments are expected within 2 quarters.

**Part 2 — Partner pays Eagle Ridge**
Mirror of Part 1, with `[Partner Referral Fee %]` on the Partner's eligible
collected revenue. *(Delete this Part if the agreement is one-directional —
see Open Decision 1.)*

**Payment prerequisites (Mercury-compatible).** No payment issues until all of:
signed agreement · registered Referral ID · Partner invoice referencing that ID ·
completed W-9 (US) or W-8 (non-US) · verified payment details on file.

## Schedule B — Referral Operations

### Lead Registration fields
Partner name · prospect company · prospect contact · consent confirmation ·
stated need and timing · source of relationship · conflict flag · submission date.

### Registration expiration
An accepted referral expires `[Registration Expiration: default 180 days]` after
acceptance unless Eagle Ridge records show active sales activity with the prospect.
Expiry ends fee eligibility for that referral only if no paid agreement was signed.

### Revenue verification
Eagle Ridge records control. Once per calendar year, Partner may request a review
limited to revenue for its own accepted referrals, conducted by an independent
accountant under NDA. **Partner bears the cost, unless the review finds an
underpayment greater than 5%, in which case Eagle Ridge pays the review cost and
the shortfall within 30 days.**

### Dispute process
Written notice within 60 days of a payout statement; good-faith resolution within
30 days; unresolved disputes escalate per §12 governing law.

### Operating ledger
Schedule B defines the **fields**; the live ledger is a separate Google Sheet,
`Eagle Ridge - Referral Operations Ledger`, linked here, with three tabs:
`Referral Registry` · `Revenue Receipts` · `Payout Ledger`.
*(Google Docs has no tabs-as-ledger; do not attempt this inside the Doc.)*
